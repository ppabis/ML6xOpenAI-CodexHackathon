# Judging Rubric

## Scoring

- Problem clarity and scope - 15 points
- AI-native workflow - 20 points
- Working implementation - 20 points
- Quality and review readiness - 20 points
- Human judgment and collaboration - 15 points
- Demo clarity and impact - 10 points

## What Judges Should Look For

- Was the problem framed clearly?
- Was the scope realistic for the available time—three hours for this project?
- Did the team use Codex throughout the lifecycle, not only for code?
- Is there a working or credible prototype?
- Did the team show testing, validation, or review evidence?
- Did the team show thoughtful human decisions?
- Was the demo easy to follow and meaningful?

## Possible Awards

- Best AI-Native Workflow
- Best Working Product
- Best Trusted PR
- Best Use of Human Judgment
- Best Demo
- Bonus: Best Workflow Accelerator

## Team Award Focus

The Codex Voice Notification Plugin targets:

1. **Best AI-Native Workflow** — show Codex supporting the full lifecycle, paired with explicit human decisions, rejected or revised outputs, and traceable artifacts.
2. **Best Working Product** — show a real installable plugin, live local speech, safe validation, structured failures, and repeatable test evidence.

Use one connected evidence chain: product brief → architecture decision → implementation change → test result → live demo. Do not count plans as implementation evidence or generated output as human validation.

### Final Evidence Checklist

- [ ] Real happy path runs from Codex without editing code.
- [ ] At least two meaningful failure or safety paths are shown.
- [ ] Automated and manual test results are recorded.
- [ ] `08-codex-workflow-log.md` spans scoping through demo preparation.
- [ ] At least one Codex output changed or rejected by a human is explained.
- [ ] Limitations and fallback behavior are stated clearly.
